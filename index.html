Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Are the listed prices final?

Prices shown are the current advertised ranges or starting prices. Final pricing is confirmed after discussing content, pages, functionality, and delivery. Birthday and party invitation websites have a fixed advertised price of ₹800 for the agreed standard scope.

Does the price include a domain and hosting?

Domain registration, renewals, paid hosting, and paid third-party services are not automatically included. Any extra costs will be confirmed before work begins.

Can I request a custom design?

Yes. Share your preferred style, content, and examples. The final scope, included revisions, and any additional cost will be agreed before work begins.

Will my feedback form store customer responses?

Only if data storage and a suitable backend or connected service are specifically implemented and included. A front-end-only form does not automatically send or store responses.

How do I get started?

Use the WhatsApp buttons on this page to tell us which service you need. We will discuss your requirements and confirm the scope before starting.
